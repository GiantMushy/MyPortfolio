UI/UX decisions (answered 2026‑09‑19)

Defaults I'll use unless you say otherwise

  • Projects tab card order: State Your Case first (newest work leads), then the current order, Woodworking last.
  • I read your "State Your Case (new!)" as enthusiasm, not a request for a literal New! ribbon on the card — if you'd actually like a little ribbon, say the word, it's cheap.
  • Job Experience cards run newest first, and the duration line mirrors LinkedIn's wording (e.g. "Jun 2024 – Present · 1 yr 4 mos").
  • Job & Team cards are clean panels — no time badges or category icons; those stay a Projects-grid thing.
  • Team photo carousels use the same arrows + dots as project pages, manual only, no auto-advance.
  • On phones the horizontal cards stack: logo (or photo carousel) on top, text below.
  • Team photos live in numbered folders exactly like project images (images/teams/angry_puffin_games/angry_puffin_games_1.jpg …) — renaming reorders them, same as everywhere else.
  • The site always opens on the Projects tab; tab choice isn't remembered between visits.
  • Hluthafaskrá's one screenshot gets deleted from the repo as instructed (git history keeps a copy forever if you ever regret it).
